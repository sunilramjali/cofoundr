How CoFoundr works today

CoFoundr is an AI co-founder for GAIA Coffee, an East London coffee truck (demo data is synthetic). It keeps track of invoices, spots what's overdue and drafts what to do about it. It never touches money until the owner says yes.

In plain English

What each piece does

Memory

MongoDB Atlas

One database, cofoundr-db. It holds the business, invoices (money owed to GAIA by customers, and money GAIA owes suppliers), the actions waiting for approval, and a log of everything the agent has done.

Brain

Gemini 4 Argon

Reads an invoice photo into who, how much, when due and line items, then checks its own work: do the lines add up, are the dates valid? If not, it looks again. If the invoice itself is wrong, it flags it for the owner instead of guessing. It also drafts reminders that get firmer each time.

Front door

Web app on Fly.io

The owner's dashboard and the endpoints Saint uses, in one app at cofoundr.fly.dev. It's the only thing that talks to Gemini and MongoDB.

Voice

Saint on WhatsApp

The owner's assistant. It asks our app for the brief and passes "approve 1" or "reject 2" back. The endpoints are ready; connecting Saint is the next test.

Walkthrough

The life of one invoice

  1. Upload. The owner adds a photo or PDF on the dashboard (WhatsApp photos come in MVP2).

  2. Read and check. Gemini extracts the fields. Code checks the line items equal the total and the dates make sense. If a check fails, Gemini re-reads once. Still wrong → needs review, confidence drops, nothing is guessed.

  3. Overdue check. Past its due date → marked overdue. Customer invoices get a reminder drafted by Gemini: friendly nudge → firm reminder → final notice, based on how many were already sent. Supplier bills get a "please pay" note for the owner.

  4. Waiting for approval. Each draft becomes a numbered pending approval with a one-line "why".

  5. Owner decides. On the dashboard (Approve / Edit / Reject) or on WhatsApp ("approve 1"). Deciding twice is refused.

  6. Applied and logged. Approved reminder → reminder count goes up (sending is simulated). Approved payment → invoice marked paid. Every step lands in agent_log, shown on the dashboard.

If Gemini is unavailable, drafts fall back to templates, so the flow still works on stage.
