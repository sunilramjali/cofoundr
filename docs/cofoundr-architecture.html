CoFoundr. how it's built

An AI co-founder for a one-truck coffee business. One FastAPI app on Fly.io sits between the owner's channels, Gemini 4 Argon and MongoDB Atlas. It does the work on its own and stops at one gate before any money moves.

The system

Every input enters the same app, every decision passes through approvals.decide(), and every step lands in agent_log. Gemini is only ever called from inside the app, and its answers are checked by code before they're stored or shown.
